\section{Conclusion}

We introduced inference-time language steering in latent space.
By extracting language directions via PCA on parallel translations, our approach enables control over language identity (reduced KL divergence by up to 55\%, Code-Switching Index by 63--99\%) with negligible overhead - a single projection.
We also show that language identity concentrates in final layers with near-perfect linear separability (96-100\% classification accuracy).
Further work remains on the stability of steering coefficients (aggressive values can produce degenerate repetition) and generalization.
