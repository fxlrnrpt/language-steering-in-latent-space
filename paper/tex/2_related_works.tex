\section{Related works}

\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Code-switching and multilingual control.}
While code-switching has been studied for mixed-language inputs in speech and NER \citep{aguilar-etal-2020-lince, bali-etal-2014-borrowing} and in LLMs \citep{zhang-etal-2023-multilingual},
recent work identifies unintended code-switching in multilingual LLM outputs \citep{ryan-etal-2024-unintended, yoo-etal-2024-csrt},
degrading user experience and evaluation.
Standard mitigations require costly fine-tuning on monolingual data \citep{marchisio-etal-2024-understanding}, reinforcement learning with language-specific rewards \citep{deepseekai2025r1}, or brittle prompt engineering \citep{marchisio-etal-2024-understanding}.

\paragraph{Language structure in representations.}
Work on mBERT and XLM-R showed language identity concentrates in few principal components, separable from semantics in parallel texts \citep{conneau-etal-2020-emerging, chi-etal-2020-finding, libovicky-etal-2020-language}.
\citet{yang-etal-2021-lir} removed these components for language-agnostic embeddings.
\citet{wendler-etal-2024-llamas} extended this to decoder-only LLMs, revealing latent language preferences, while language-specific neurons concentrate in the first and last layers \citep{tang-etal-2024-language, kojima-etal-2024-multilingual, zhao2024multilingualism}.
Language-specific subspaces \citep{zhao2024lens} and sparse autoencoder features \citep{deng-etal-2025-unveiling} have also been used to enhance or steer multilingual generation.
We exploit this structure for active generation control.

\paragraph{Representation steering and concept erasure.}
Recent work has shown that high-level concepts can be located and manipulated in model representations via linear methods \citep{park2024linear}.
\citet{zou2023representationengineering} introduced representation engineering, using PCA on contrastive prompt pairs to identify concept directions (e.g., honesty, harmlessness) and steering activations along them.
\citet{turner2024steeringlanguagemodelsactivation} proposed activation addition, adding steering vectors to the residual stream at inference time, later extended to contrastive activation addition \citep{rimsky-etal-2024-steering}.
In parallel, concept erasure methods like \citet{ravfogel-etal-2020-nullitout} (iterative null-space projection) and ~\citet{belrose2023leace} (closed-form linear erasure) remove specific attributes from representations by projecting onto their null space.
\citet{li2024iti} applied probing-guided inference-time intervention on attention heads to elicit truthful outputs.
These methods have primarily targeted sentiment, truthfulness, and toxicity.
We apply the same conceptual framework to language identity.
